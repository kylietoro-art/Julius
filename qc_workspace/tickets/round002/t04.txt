[P1] Real-World Facts, Institutions and Jurisdiction Stated Correctly (consolidated)

The world fails the "No Synthetic Substitutes For Real-World Facts" clause because it repeatedly assigns California's incapacity exception for late government claims to Government Code § 911.6(b)(4). In `task_fs/filesystem/Legal/Reyes/late_claim_app.pdf`, the application says, "Relief is sought principally under Government Code § 911.6(b)(4)" and later says the facts establish incapacity "within the meaning of Government Code § 911.6(b)(4)." The selected world specification also defines the capacity-adverse facts as those that "defeat § 911.6(b)(4) 'all of the time,'" and `Legal/Reyes/intake_memo.docx` calls that subsection the incapacity ground. Because § 911.6(b)(4) is the death-of-claimant provision while incapacity is § 911.6(b)(3), this is a repeated, core California-law error in the world's late-claim strategy; replace each incapacity reference with § 911.6(b)(3) and revise the related explanation.
