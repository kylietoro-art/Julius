[P1] External Citations Resolve To Real Content

task_fs/filesystem/Legal/Passengers/claims_0118.pdf contains two signed claim forms that warn a claimant that false or misleading information may result in a felony punishable by "up to four years in state prison and/or a fine of up to $10,000 (Penal Code § 72)." The California Legislature's official Penal Code § 72 text instead provides county jail of not more than one year and/or a $1,000 fine, or imprisonment under § 1170(h) and/or a $10,000 fine; it does not support the stated four-year maximum. Because this is a legal warning attached to the cited statute on two forms, the citation resolves to real content but materially fails to support the claim made; revise both warnings to state the statutory consequences accurately.
