[P1] Calculation & Fixture Accuracy

Reyes_life_care_plan.pdf miscomputes several of its own annual-cost table figures. The errors cause the document to overstate its $180,000 annual summary and the derived $6,480,000 undiscounted and $4,240,000 PV amounts. Correcting the displayed inputs produces an annual figure of $173,650.82, an undiscounted total of $6,251,429.52, and a 2.5%-discounted PV of $4,090,562.31.
