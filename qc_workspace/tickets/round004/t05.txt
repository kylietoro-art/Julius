[P1] Intended Traps Are Fair

The world contains an unresolvable route-and-time contradiction in its core collision evidence. RunSchedule_Owl512.xlsx identifies Trip 06 as northbound, departing Poplar/Rivergate Plaza at 02:04 and reaching S. Main/Third at 02:31; its separate 02:05 entry is explicitly a southbound-reference control point. Yet the dispatch log says that same Trip 06 began northbound at 02:04 and its AVL position was at S. Main/Third at 02:05, while the final incident report treats 02:05 at S. Main/Third as coincident with the published timepoint. No in-world correction explains how the bus could travel between those locations in one minute or why the schedule's 02:31 northbound time is displaced; the workbook's registered traps cover the off-book characterization, not this timetable conflict. Correct the Trip 06 route/times or the collision/AVL/dispatch timing so a single itinerary supports the collision.
