[P1] Consistent Naming And Defined Terms

The police collision report and scene-photo log call the location/signage "MTA/ACTD," but the governing world specification names Alder Creek Transit District (ACTD) as the core transit entity and defines "the District" as ACTD; it never introduces MTA. Because this creates an unexplained second transit-organization label in evidence about the collision location, it is material naming drift rather than an ordinary abbreviation. Replace "MTA/ACTD" in both police documents with the intended ACTD designation, or introduce and consistently explain MTA if it is genuinely a separate entity.
