* [P1] Consistent Naming And Defined TermsMinor
The City of Rivergate property-demand letter calls the police record "RPD Case No. 2025-1018-0037," and the supporting repair invoice repeats that label as the "RPD Traffic Collision Report." In contrast, the report itself identifies its local report number as "TC-2025-1018," and the criminal docket and production tracker use that same identifier. Because the City materials purport to identify the same RPD report rather than their own City file, the unreconciled change creates real confusion; the smallest fix is to use TC-2025-1018 in the City documents or add an explicit cross-reference if 2025-1018-0037 is a genuine secondary number.
