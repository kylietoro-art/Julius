[P1] Consistent Naming And Defined Terms

The dispatch log states that "Incident number ACTD-IR-2025-1018-001 [was] opened" and later that the same suffixed "Incident file" remained open. In contrast, the final safety report certifies itself as the "Final Incident Report of record for Incident No. ACTD-IR-2025-1018," and the onboard video still log identifies that shorter number as its incident reference. Neither source identifies either string as an alias or parent/child record, so a worker searching, requesting, or cross-referencing the incident file can reasonably be left unsure whether the records belong to one collision; standardize the incident number or state the relationship explicitly.
