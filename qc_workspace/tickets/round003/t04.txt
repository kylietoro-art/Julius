[P1] Real-World Facts, Institutions and Jurisdiction Stated Correctly (consolidated)

The world fails the "No Synthetic Substitutes For Real-World Facts" clause because ACTD's written policy asserts that 49 CFR § 655.44 conditions nonfatal post-accident testing on specified damage, injury/citation, hazmat, or immovable-object circumstances. The regulation's actual nonfatal rule requires testing after an accident involving a public transportation vehicle unless the employee's performance can be completely discounted, so the policy both adds non-regulatory requirements and may unlawfully suppress testing. This is a material factual misstatement of a named federal framework in a U.S.-centered public-transit litigation world; revise § 3.2 to track 49 CFR § 655.44(a)(2) and remove the FMCSA-like criteria.

Finding #1: ACTD policy misstates FTA nonfatal post-accident testing rule. Major.
