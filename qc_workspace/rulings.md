# Standing rulings

Decisions that bind every later round. Read these before triage.
Never re-ask a question answered here.

- **2026-09-16** (round 2): Internal memos and filed advocacy documents are allowed to draw their own conclusions; that alone is not leakage. Only strip content that pre-computes another task's specific deliverable values (dates/figures) or that a non-lawyer author has no business asserting as settled law.

- **2026-09-16** (round 3): A document's own conclusions are only protected when the document is allowed to reach them (an internal memo, filed advocacy) or the conclusion is outside its author's authority to assert; strip a conclusion when the document itself is the neutral operational record a specific task's failure mode turns on (e.g. IR_2025-1018_final.docx and T5) rather than an advocacy document modeling one side's read of the evidence.
